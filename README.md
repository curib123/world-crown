# WORLD CROWN

Standalone story-development repository for the WORLD CROWN historical-fantasy tournament novel.

## Core premise
The World Crown appears over Earth and compels each participating national entry to choose one champion from anywhere in its history or present age. Death and era are not barriers. The tournament begins as national survival and ultimately becomes a conflict over whether one civilization has the right to erase all others.

## Story identity
- Ensemble / multiple-protagonist narrative.
- Roughly 8–12 recurring core protagonists from different nations and eras.
- One champion per participating nation.
- Historical and modern fighters coexist.
- Every champion has a unique Crown Title and individualized combat system.
- No protagonist nation and no guaranteed finalist.
- National elimination triggers National Collapse.
- The hidden end-state is one surviving nation.
- The final conflict becomes Humanity vs. the World Crown.

## Repository structure
- `00_PROJECT/` — governance and authority rules
- `01_CANON/` — locked canon and narrative rules
- `02_CHAMPIONS/` — champion and ensemble structure
- `03_WORLD/` — World Crown, National Collapse, institutions, history
- `04_POWER_SYSTEMS/` — combat-system rules
- `05_TOURNAMENT/` — format, pacing, brackets, match logic
- `06_REFERENCES/` — historical research policy
- `07_TEMPLATES/` — reusable champion and match templates
- `08_PLANNING/` — saga and long-range planning
- `09_FUTURE_STORY/` — ideas not yet canon
- `10_QA/` — continuity, canon, prose, and structural QA
- `11_STORY_STUDIO/` — development workspace
- `12_BOOKS/` — manuscript containers
- `WEBNOVEL_EXPORT/` — publication-ready exports
- `docs/` — project documentation and approved specs
- `scripts/` — future automation

This repository intentionally contains no canon or manuscript from *Survive as a Reincarnated Bat*.
