# WORLD CROWN — Master Canon

## Premise
The World Crown appears and commands every participating national entry to choose one champion from anywhere in its history or present day. Death, era, and chronology do not block selection.

## National Rule
Each participating nation has exactly one active champion. A champion represents a nation but remains an independent person with their own values, motives, regrets, and interpretation of homeland.

## The Culling
The tournament is part of the Culling of Nations. A nation that is permanently eliminated enters National Collapse: instability, environmental and infrastructural failure, spatial loss, mass evacuation, and eventual territorial erasure. Citizens may survive as refugees.

## Hidden Final Rule
Humanity initially believes surviving enough rounds can save a nation. The deeper truth is that the World Crown intends to continue until only one nation is guaranteed to remain.

## Ensemble Canon
There is no single permanent main character and no permanently privileged protagonist nation. The story follows roughly 8–12 recurring primary protagonists from different nations, supported by other champions, delegations, civilians, historians, officials, spectators, and eliminated fighters.

Two primary protagonists may fight each other. A protagonist may lose, die, be eliminated, or lose their homeland.

## Crown Titles
Every champion receives a unique Crown Title representing the historical or legendary idea most strongly associated with them.

## Combat Identity
Every champion is effectively their own combat system, built from Human Art, Crown Body, Regalia, Legacy Trait, Signature Arts, Legend Authority, Crown Resonance, and rare Crown Revelation.

## Escalation
The story begins as a national survival tournament and evolves through rivalry, friendship, ideological fracture, previous Crown cycles, the one-nation revelation, and finally open conflict against the World Crown system.

## Thematic Questions
- What makes a nation worth preserving?
- What separates history from legend?
- Is victory still victory if everyone else must lose their homeland?
- Can cultural identity survive without domination?
- Who has the right to decide which civilizations deserve to continue?
