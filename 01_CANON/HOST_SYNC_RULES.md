# WORLD CROWN - Host Sync Rules

## Core Rule

Host Sync is the World Crown's controlled use of Soul Drift. It does not resurrect or recreate a champion's true body. For a deceased or historical champion, it anchors the champion's drifting soul inside a living host, carrying the champion's memories, trained instincts, identity, Crown Title, and legend into a body that already belongs to another soul.

The host is not an empty vessel. Host and champion remain distinct persons with separate souls, memories, fears, loyalties, physical histories, and rights to the host body. The sync creates a pressured partnership that can become cooperation, conflict, partial blending of sensation, or refusal; it does not automatically merge their personalities.

## Who Enters

- Deceased or historical champions use Soul Drift and Host Sync through a living compatible host connected to the national entry.
- Living champions may enter as self-hosted participants; the Crown synchronizes their present body with the Crown combat state without replacing them with another body.
- A host candidate may volunteer, be selected by a government or delegation, be chosen by Crown Compatibility, or resist the selection. Consent affects stability but does not erase the Crown's coercive nature.

## Sync Layers

1. Contact: the host experiences the champion's presence, memories, habits, or battlefield reflexes.
2. Anchoring: the Crown binds both souls to the host body for tournament participation.
3. Overlay: Crown Body enhancements and Human Art become accessible, but timing and control remain imperfect.
4. Alignment: host and champion negotiate movement, motive, pain, and the meaning of the Crown Title.
5. Resonance: combat becomes more precise when both souls act toward a compatible conviction.

## Agency and Limits

- The host can resist a movement, memory, command, or Crown interpretation when the sync is incomplete.
- The champion cannot simply erase the host soul or claim the host body as property.
- The host's physical condition, injuries, habits, and limits remain relevant to combat.
- Conflicting goals create latency, misread movement, memory intrusion, emotional bleed, or temporary desync.
- High power without host alignment is unstable and carries a cost.
- A champion's historical skill is not automatically the host's practiced skill; Soul Drift must teach, share, or negotiate it under pressure.
- Host injury affects the living body and may create consequences beyond tournament victory.

## Narrative Purpose

Host Sync makes identity a relationship rather than a costume. It creates immediate conflict between historical reputation and present personhood, gives modern civilians a role in the tournament, and prevents a champion's accomplishments from becoming a consequence-free resurrection.

Every Host Sync must track host soul and identity, champion soul and identity, compatibility, consent or refusal, first conflict, shared capability, control boundary, sync cost, and the relationship's direction after each major battle.

## Prohibited Shortcuts

- Do not call a Host Sync a resurrection, reconstruction of the true body, or cloned historical body.
- Do not treat the host as disposable equipment or a silent audience.
- Do not let sync create unrelated powers that neither identity established.
- Do not resolve host/champion conflict through instant personality deletion.
