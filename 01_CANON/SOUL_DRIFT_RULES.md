# WORLD CROWN - Soul Drift

## Definition

Soul Drift is the story's natural reincarnation mechanism. A soul can separate from its body after biological death and, under rare conditions, cross between realms or worlds. Soul Drift is not ordinary resurrection: the dead body does not return, and the drifting soul must find or be anchored to a living existence.

## Two Souls, One Body

When the World Crown uses Soul Drift for a historical champion, two people occupy one living host body:

- the modern host, whose soul, memories, personality, relationships, body, and agency remain real;
- the historical champion, whose drifting soul carries personal memory, trained instincts, identity, Crown Title, and the weight of later legend.

They are two personalities in one body, not one personality wearing another person's body. Neither soul automatically replaces, deletes, or owns the other.

## Host Sync

Host Sync is the World Crown's deliberate method of finding, crossing to, and anchoring a compatible champion soul inside a living host. It weaponizes a rare natural phenomenon for the Culling of Nations. The Crown can stabilize the shared body and make combat possible, but it cannot make the two souls identical or guarantee cooperation.

Living champions may be self-hosted: their own soul remains in their own body while the Crown amplifies the body's combat state. Self-hosted champions do not receive a second personality unless another Soul Drift event is explicitly established.

## Drift Stages

1. Separation: the champion's soul leaves the biological body after death.
2. Crossing: the soul moves through time, realm, or world under rare conditions.
3. Contact: a compatible living host senses another presence, memory, voice, or instinct.
4. Anchoring: the Crown binds both souls to the host body for tournament participation.
5. Sync: the souls learn to share sensation, movement, memory, and decision under pressure.
6. Drift Event: stress, injury, conviction, legend, or Crown interference can shift which soul is closer to the body's surface without erasing the other.

## Agency and Conflict

- The host can resist, negotiate, or refuse a movement, memory, command, or Crown interpretation.
- The champion can disagree with the host's values, era, relationships, language, and reason for fighting.
- Both souls can perceive the body, but their access to sensation and movement may differ.
- Conflict can create delayed reactions, competing speech, involuntary memories, emotional bleed, sensory overload, or temporary loss of coordination.
- Cooperation can create shared technique, complementary perception, and a new conviction neither soul could reach alone.
- The host's physical condition and the champion's soul condition both limit combat.
- A Soul Drift does not make the host a disposable vessel, puppet, reincarnation shell, or silent observer.

## Identity and Legend

The Crown may draw power from the champion's history and legend, but the host changes how that legend is embodied. A Crown Title can create conflict between what the world expects the champion to be and what the host will permit the shared body to become. Crown Resonance therefore includes both souls, the host body, public legend, and present conviction.

## Prohibited Interpretations

- Do not resurrect the champion's true body.
- Do not imply that the host soul was absent, inferior, or waiting to be overwritten.
- Do not treat Soul Drift as instant possession with no relationship or cost.
- Do not resolve the two-person conflict through unexplained personality deletion.
- Do not use Soul Drift to create unrelated powers or undo meaningful death.
