# WORLD CROWN - Character Arc Framework

## Purpose

Champion development is a change in belief, relationship, identity, or choice. It is not a reward track and must not be reduced to a new attack.

## Required Arc Fields

Every core protagonist profile tracks these fields:

- Starting Belief: what the champion believes about nation, victory, history, or people at selection;
- Personal Wound / Regret: the loss, failure, guilt, fear, or unresolved responsibility shaping present choices;
- Historical Contradiction: the gap between the person, the record, and the later legend;
- Relationship Anchor: the person, group, homeland, or rival that makes the arc emotionally specific;
- First Challenge: the first event that tests the Starting Belief;
- Midpoint Crisis: the result, discovery, or betrayal that makes the old belief costly to maintain;
- Decisive Choice: the choice made when victory and identity cannot both remain unchanged;
- Consequence: what the choice costs the champion and the people connected to them;
- End State: the belief, relationship, responsibility, or unresolved truth left after the arc.

## Match Integration

A match should pressure at least one arc field and produce a visible change. The change may be a loss, surrender, failure, refusal, new alliance, altered relationship, public correction, survival, or decision to continue. The next chapter records the new state rather than treating the result as an isolated spectacle.

## Ensemble Requirements

Each core champion needs one rivalry, one meaningful bond, one ideological conflict, one relationship changed by a match result, and one connection to civilians, refugees, or homeland consequences. Before two recurring protagonists fight, establish meaningful relationship material unless first-contact shock is deliberately the point.

## Loss and Elimination

Narrative importance does not guarantee advancement. A defeated champion may complete an arc through how they lose, what they protect, whom they trust, what truth they reveal, or what they do after elimination. A champion may remain relevant as an analyst, mentor, investigator, dissenter, refugee advocate, Crown rebel, ally, or adversary.

## QA

Do not lock a core champion without identifying all nine arc fields, the match pressure on the arc, the changed relationship, and the consequence that continues after victory or defeat.
