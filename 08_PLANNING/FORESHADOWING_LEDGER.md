# WORLD CROWN - Foreshadowing Ledger

Use one row or entry for every medium or major seed. Minor seeds may be grouped when they resolve in the same chapter or match.

## Entry Fields

- Seed ID:
- Mystery / reveal:
- Scale: minor / medium / major
- Seed text or event:
- Category: behavioral / visual / dialogue / historical / mechanical / relational / thematic
- First appearance: chapter, match, or saga
- First interpretation:
- Reinforcement:
- Misdirection, if used:
- Reveal location:
- Payoff:
- Consequence:
- Responsible chapter / match:
- Status: planted / reinforced / revealed / paid off / consequence delivered

## Review Rules

- The first interpretation must be reasonable using information available at the time.
- Reinforcement must add meaning or pressure rather than repeat the same clue.
- Misdirection cannot require the story to lie about a fact already shown.
- The reveal must preserve established power and Crown rules unless the hidden rule was itself seeded.
- The payoff must reinterpret or use an earlier detail.
- The consequence must change a future decision, relationship, tactic, public belief, or mystery path.

## Example - Non-Canon Training Entry

This example is a format demonstration only and is not part of locked WORLD CROWN history.

- Seed ID: EX-001
- Mystery / reveal: Why does the example champion refuse the bronze bell?
- Scale: minor
- Seed text or event: In the opening chapter, Example Champion touches the bell's rim, then withdraws before the sound is made.
- Category: behavioral / visual
- First appearance: Example Chapter 01
- First interpretation: The bell is a ceremonial object the champion dislikes.
- Reinforcement: In Example Chapter 02, the champion changes the subject when a witness mentions its previous owner.
- Misdirection, if used: A spectator assumes the champion fears bad luck.
- Reveal location: Example Chapter 03
- Payoff: The bell's tone would expose the champion's injured hearing and change how their Human Art is used.
- Consequence: The opponent stops treating the champion's silence as arrogance and changes the opening tactic.
- Responsible chapter / match: Example Match A
- Status: consequence delivered
