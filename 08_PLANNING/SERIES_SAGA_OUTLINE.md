# WORLD CROWN — Series Saga Outline

Target scale: roughly 500–650+ chapters. Ranges are planning guides, not quotas.

## Saga I — The Crown Descends | Ch. 1–30
World Crown appears; Culling announced; National Records; reconstruction proven; several core protagonists introduced. End: registration closes.

## Saga II — Champions of Humanity | Ch. 31–80
Major champions arrive; Crown Titles and Regalia introduced; historical/modern interaction; rivalries and friendships form. End: groups revealed.

## Saga III — First Blood | Ch. 81–150
Group stage begins; Human Art, Crown Body, Regalia, Legacy dominate; standings and strategic concealment matter. End: first major Legend Authority shocks the world.

## Saga IV — Legends Collide | Ch. 151–220
Signature Arts and counter-strategy deepen; relationships strengthen; first formal eliminations; first National Collapse. End: Final 32 confirmed.

## Saga V — The Falling World | Ch. 221–250
Slower aftermath; refugees; defeated champions; desperate governments; Crown research; Legend Distortion. End: knockout bracket opens.

## Saga VI — Round of 32 | Ch. 251–340
Single elimination; sophisticated preparation; core protagonists may face each other; recurring protagonists can fall. End: 16 remain.

## Saga VII — Sixteen Legends | Ch. 341–410
High Resonance; selective True Regalia; survivors become global icons; prior Crown cycles gain credible confirmation; rebellion debate begins. End: 8 remain.

## Saga VIII — Sovereign Eight | Ch. 411–480
Every match is a major arc; deepest historical/personal material pays off; first earned Revelations; previous civilizations linked to Crown cycles. End: 4 remain.

## Saga IX — Four Flags | Ch. 481–530
One-nation truth revealed; surviving protagonists split philosophically; semifinals test both mechanics and beliefs. End: 2 finalists.

## Saga X — The Last Match | Ch. 531–570
Strongest pure 1v1; both finalists treated as protagonists; all established layers matter. End: one champion wins and the Crown opens.

## Saga XI — The True Crown | Ch. 571–600
Deeper Crown history, previous cycles, system mechanics, National Collapse logic, and final-collapse preparation are revealed.

## Saga XII — Divided Legends | Ch. 601–625
Survivors and eliminated champions choose sides; friendships fracture; fights occur outside bracket rules. End: factions converge on the Crown.

## Saga XIII — Crown War | Ch. 626+
Former enemies cooperate or settle ideological conflict; team battles; modern/historical champions fight side by side; humanity confronts the system deciding which civilizations may continue.

### Thematic movement
“My nation must survive.”
→ “My nation must win.”
→ “What does winning cost?”
→ “No system has the right to erase everyone else.”
