## Major Twist Gate

Before approving a major twist, answer all six questions:

1. What prior seeds make it fair?
2. Which earlier scenes gain a new meaning?
3. What future choice changes because of it?
4. What emotional consequence cannot be ignored?
5. Which established rule does it preserve, or which seeded hidden rule does it reveal?
6. What meaningful payoff follows instead of a temporary shock?

Major twists may include previous-cycle proof, distorted historical identity, a conflicting ally agenda, Herald manipulation within Crown law, a sympathetic antagonist inversion, an ally choosing homeland over the ensemble, the one-nation end-state, or victory triggering the final mechanism. Do not use a twist to undo a meaningful death, sacrifice, elimination, or National Collapse merely to surprise the reader.
