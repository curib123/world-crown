# Story Studio Workflow

1. Develop an idea in `11_STORY_STUDIO/`, `08_PLANNING/`, or `09_FUTURE_STORY/`.
2. Check historical claims in `06_REFERENCES/` if real people/events are involved.
3. Check canon compatibility with `01_CANON/`.
4. Check power compatibility with `04_POWER_SYSTEMS/`.
5. Check tournament/pacing consequences with `05_TOURNAMENT/`.
6. Run `10_QA/CANON_QA_CHECKLIST.md`.
7. Promote only the approved parts into canon/champion/tournament files.
8. Draft manuscript only after the relevant scene/match foundation is stable.
9. Never let draft prose silently redefine canon.

Story development should preserve uncertainty in the bracket, relationship continuity, fair-play mysteries, and consequences after victory or defeat.
