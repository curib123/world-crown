# World Crown and National Collapse

## The World Crown
A supernatural/cosmic system, structure, or entity appears over Earth and initiates the Culling of Nations.

It recognizes its own tournament field of national entries. Its recognition is a fictional supernatural rule and should not be presented as a definitive real-world legal statement about sovereignty.

## National Record
Each participating nation gains access to a supernatural National Record containing people the Crown recognizes as eligible from that nation's historical lineage or present age.

Selection can involve government choice, public pressure, Crown Compatibility, refusal, political dispute, or champion consent depending on later canon.

## Host Sync
Dead historical figures are not resurrected and their true bodies are not recreated. The World Crown selects a living compatible host and synchronizes the historical champion's identity, memory-pattern, trained instincts, Crown Title, and legend through the host's own body. Host and champion remain separate persons; the host may consent, resist, negotiate, or be changed by the sync. Living champions may enter as self-hosted participants. Time and death do not prevent participation, but every historical entry requires a living host relationship.

## National Collapse
Collapse begins only after formal tournament elimination, not after an ordinary group-stage loss.

Default stages:
1. Crown Protection Lost — instability begins.
2. Resource / Environmental Failure — systems and environment deteriorate.
3. Structural / Spatial Collapse — territory and infrastructure become physically unstable.
4. Exodus — population attempts mass evacuation.
5. Erasure — homeland territory disappears.

Citizens may survive as refugees even after homeland erasure.

## Hidden Purpose
Humanity initially believes the tournament decides which nations survive long enough. The Crown's deeper intention is to reduce civilization toward one surviving national/civilizational foundation.

## Previous Cycles
Evidence later suggests similar Crown cycles affected earlier civilizations. This should be revealed gradually, not stated openly at the beginning.

## Endgame Moral Conflict
The tournament eventually forces champions to confront whether saving one's own homeland can justify accepting the destruction of every other homeland.
