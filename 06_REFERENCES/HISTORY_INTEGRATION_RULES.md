# WORLD CROWN - History Integration Rules

## Evidence Labels

Every historical statement in a champion profile, chapter plan, flashback, battle plan, or research note uses one of five labels:

1. Documented history - supported by reliable surviving records or strong scholarly consensus.
2. Disputed history - plausible but contested, incomplete, or dependent on conflicting sources.
3. Later legend - a story, reputation, cultural memory, or heroic image that developed after the person's life.
4. Crown interpretation - a supernatural reading or manifestation created by the World Crown from history, reputation, or legend.
5. Deliberate fiction - an invented event, relationship, object, or alteration used by the novel.

The fifth label never disguises invention as authenticated fact. The Crown may react to legend, but the narrative must still show that legend and documented history are different things.

## Dramatic Use of History

History enters through a present dramatic question. Use memory, argument, habits, scars, weapons, tactics, values, conflicting accounts, modern people meeting the historical person, historians, ruins, testimony, propaganda, or the champion's response to being remembered.

Before adding a flashback, state what present conflict it answers. A flashback should reveal character, belief, technique, title, trauma, contradiction, motivation, or a cost that matters now. Avoid chronological biography summaries that pause the story without changing a decision.

## Accomplishments and Titles

Champion Presentation separates:

- verified accomplishment: what the person demonstrably did;
- disputed accomplishment: what may have happened or is interpreted differently;
- legendary accomplishment: what later culture says the person did;
- Crown manifestation: what capability the Crown builds from those layers.

Crown Titles condense the strongest historical or legendary idea associated with a champion. A Title does not authorize unrelated spectacle. Human Art, Regalia, Legacy Trait, Signature Arts, Legend Authority, and any Revelation must grow from the labeled identity record.

## Weapons and Regalia

If an exact historical weapon or object is unknown, call the Regalia a Crown reconstruction or symbolic manifestation. Never present an invented object as authenticated history. Its property must still be distinct, limited, and tied to documented practice, disputed account, later legend, or a clearly labeled Crown interpretation.

## Contradiction and Personhood

Champions are people first and symbols second. Let them disagree with modern governments, reject nationalism, resent public memory, correct false stories, or recognize that their own legacy caused harm. Do not sanitize serious harm or turn a complex figure into a caricature solely to make the battle morally simple.

## Research Record

Every locked profile keeps sources and uncertainty visible. When a fact cannot be verified, write the uncertainty into the profile and design the scene around the character's relationship to that uncertainty rather than silently upgrading it to fact.
