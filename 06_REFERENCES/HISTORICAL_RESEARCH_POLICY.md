# Historical Research Policy

WORLD CROWN uses real historical people alongside deliberate fantasy. Research must distinguish:
1. documented history;
2. disputed or uncertain history;
3. later legend / folklore;
4. Crown interpretation inside the fictional setting;
5. deliberate fictionalization.

## Rules
- Research real historical figures before locking profiles or powers.
- Do not present uncertain personal weapons, quotes, motives, or events as verified fact.
- Do not sanitize major atrocities, conquest, persecution, or documented harms to make a fighter easier to like.
- Do not reduce controversial people to caricatures either; show evidence, historical context, and character complexity.
- Powers may draw from legend, but the project should know when it is using legend rather than history.
- Crown Titles are fictional epithets unless they are explicitly documented historical titles.
- Pre-modern people should not automatically think in modern national categories.

## In-story integration
Historical material should appear through memory, behavior, argument, combat habits, conflicting records, modern reactions, triggered flashbacks, and character relationships. Avoid textbook biography dumps.

## Research status labels
Champion research notes should mark major claims as `documented`, `disputed`, `legendary`, or `fictionalized` when ambiguity matters.
