# WORLD CROWN — Project Overview

## Purpose
WORLD CROWN is a long-form web novel / light novel built as an ensemble, multiple-protagonist tournament epic.

## Core Hook
Every participating national entry selects one champion from any point in its history or present day. Time and death are not barriers. Ancient warriors, rulers, rebels, martial artists, soldiers, strategists, and modern fighters can all enter.

Each champion receives a unique Crown Title and an individual combat system derived from genuine skill, historical reputation, cultural memory, and legend.

## Main Stakes
The World Crown begins the Culling of Nations. When a nation is formally eliminated, National Collapse begins. If the collapse completes, that homeland disappears. Citizens may survive as refugees.

The hidden final rule is that only one nation is guaranteed to remain.

## Narrative Model
There is no single permanent main character. WORLD CROWN follows roughly 8–12 recurring primary protagonists from different nations while allowing important match arcs to center other champions.

## Series Identity
- 195 World Crown-recognized national entries as the provisional global scale.
- One champion per participating nation.
- Past and present fighters coexist.
- Rotating close third-person limited POV.
- No protagonist plot armor.
- Every champion has a Crown Title.
- Every champion has a distinct combat identity.
- Tournament progression drives the early and middle story.
- The final conflict expands beyond nation-versus-nation into humanity versus the World Crown system.
