# Authority and Change Rules

## Canon Priority
When documents conflict, use this order:
1. `01_CANON/MASTER_CANON.md`
2. `01_CANON/NARRATIVE_STRUCTURE.md`
3. `04_POWER_SYSTEMS/MASTER_POWER_SYSTEM.md`
4. `05_TOURNAMENT/FORMAT_AND_PACING.md`
5. `08_PLANNING/SERIES_SAGA_OUTLINE.md`
6. champion-specific files and match plans
7. draft prose

## Locked Foundation
- No single permanent main character.
- Ensemble / multiple-protagonist narrative.
- One champion per participating nation.
- Champions may come from any historical era or the present.
- Every champion has a unique Crown Title.
- Every champion has a distinct combat system.
- Formal national elimination triggers National Collapse.
- The hidden end-state is one surviving nation.
- The story later escalates into conflict with the World Crown itself.

## Change Discipline
A new idea must not silently overwrite existing canon. When changing locked canon, identify the old rule, define the replacement, update dependent files, and run QA.

## Historical Claims
Separate documented facts, disputed claims, legends, and fictional reinterpretations. A power may draw from legend without presenting folklore as verified history.

## Power Continuity
New techniques must derive from established Human Art, Crown Body, Regalia, Legacy Trait, Signature Arts, Legend Authority, Crown Resonance, or previously foreshadowed Crown Revelation.

## Outcome Discipline
No champion is protected because they are part of the core ensemble. Outcomes follow matchup logic, established mechanics, psychology, choices, and cost.
