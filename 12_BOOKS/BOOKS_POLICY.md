# Books Policy

- Manuscript files live under `12_BOOKS/`.
- Planning documents do not become manuscript automatically.
- Published/export text is generated from approved manuscript, not vice versa.
- Each book/volume should identify its chapter range, sagas/arcs covered, continuity dependencies, and current lock status.
- Revisions must preserve locked outcomes and canon unless the canon change is approved separately.
- Writing style must follow the current approved prose/writing rules once implemented.
