# WORLD CROWN - Prose Style QA

Run this review on every finished chapter before it is locked or exported.

## Chapter Completion

- [ ] Chapter contains at least 1,000 meaningful words of narrative prose.
- [ ] Word count excludes title, notes, metadata, author notes, and QA text.
- [ ] Word minimum is met without padding, repetition, or artificial fragmentation.
- [ ] Chapter has one dominant POV per scene.
- [ ] Chapter contains a local objective, pressure, conflict, or meaningful decision.
- [ ] Chapter contains emotional movement or changed understanding.
- [ ] Chapter ends with a visible change.
- [ ] Chapter can be understood and emotionally felt without requiring the next chapter to finish its local scene.
- [ ] If this is an opening chapter, it contains active stakes rather than setup-only exposition.
- [ ] If this is a battle chapter, it contains direct pressure and a tactical or emotional turn.

## Continuity and Foreshadowing

- [ ] Prior-chapter payoff is present or the chapter is an intentional first-chapter setup.
- [ ] Changed state is carried into the next chapter or next planned scene.
- [ ] At least one forward seed is recorded in the foreshadowing ledger.
- [ ] Seed category and first interpretation are identified.
- [ ] Any reveal has a tracked payoff and consequence.
- [ ] Plot twist evidence is fair and does not depend on impossible reader knowledge.

## Voice and POV

- [ ] Narration stays near the POV character's perceptions, assumptions, vocabulary, and attention.
- [ ] POV switches are marked at a permitted boundary.
- [ ] Recurring character voices differ through habits, education, rank, temperament, era, and relationship.
- [ ] Narration does not state information the POV character cannot know without a deliberate framing device.

## Emotion and Dialogue

- [ ] Emotion appears through perception, behavior, body, speech, silence, memory, misunderstanding, and choice.
- [ ] Important relationships develop through actual conversation or meaningful interaction.
- [ ] Dialogue contains subtext, disagreement, interruption, humor, evasion, or silence where appropriate.
- [ ] Dialogue is not exposition disguised as conversation.
- [ ] Memorable lines arise from character truth rather than being inserted as slogans.

## Rhythm and Language

- [ ] Sentence and paragraph lengths vary naturally.
- [ ] Cinematic compression is reserved for moments that benefit from it.
- [ ] Repeated constructions, rhetorical questions, forced aphorisms, and one-line fragments are limited.
- [ ] The chapter avoids constant cliffhanger endings and excessive em-dash dependence.
- [ ] Action, description, and explanation are balanced for the scene's purpose.
- [ ] The chapter sustains the fast mythic tournament atmosphere without becoming empty spectacle.
- [ ] Recovery or exposition scenes contain their own decision, conflict, or consequence.

## History and Battle

- [ ] Historical material answers a present dramatic question.
- [ ] Documented history, disputed history, later legend, Crown interpretation, and deliberate fiction are distinguishable.
- [ ] Champion Presentation establishes both fighters before the first meaningful exchange.
- [ ] Accomplishments, Crown Titles, capabilities, and limitations are dramatized rather than dumped.
- [ ] Human Art, tactics, psychology, terrain, and counterplay remain relevant.
- [ ] No unrelated emergency power appears.
