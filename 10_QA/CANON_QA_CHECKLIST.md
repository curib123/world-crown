# WORLD CROWN — Canon QA Checklist

Run before locking a champion, match, saga change, or chapter batch.

## Canon
- Agrees with `MASTER_CANON.md`?
- Preserves ensemble structure and no hero nation?
- Preserves one champion per participating nation?
- Respects National Collapse and hidden one-nation end-state?

## POV
- One clear dominant viewpoint per scene?
- Clean POV switches?
- Major matches make both fighters meaningful?
- No protagonist plot armor?

## History
- Documented fact separated from disputed claim, legend, and fiction?
- Cultural/historical connection checked?
- Uncertain weapons presented as reconstruction rather than authenticated fact?
- Serious harms neither sanitized nor caricatured?

## Crown Title / Power
- Title memorable and grounded?
- Human Art remains relevant?
- Regalia/Legacy/Arts derive from identity?
- Authority has power, condition, limitation, counterplay?
- Revelation earned and foreshadowed?
- No unrelated emergency power?

## Match
- Why are these fighters paired narratively?
- What does each want beyond victory?
- Is the result earned by mechanics, strategy, psychology, and cost?
- Does the match change standings, relationships, character, public perception, national stakes, Crown knowledge, or future strategy?

## Pacing
- Enough breathing room?
- Flashback necessary now?
- History dramatized rather than dumped?
- Powers revealed at the right speed?
- Late abilities foreshadowed?

## Chapter Continuity
- At least 1,000 meaningful words of narrative prose, excluding titles, notes, metadata, and QA text?
- Complete standalone dramatic unit with local pressure, emotional movement, and closing change?
- Prior-chapter payoff recorded?
- Next-chapter handoff and changed state recorded?
- Forward foreshadowing seed classified and entered in the ledger?
- No padding, repetitive exposition, or artificial fragmentation?

## Champion Presentation
- Both fighters introduced before the first meaningful exchange?
- Arrival mode distinct from neighboring major battles?
- Arrival staging, POV, emotional register, and reveal order varied?
- Name, national entry, era, reason for fighting, and personhood clear?
- Crown Title and historical/legendary meaning clear?
- Verified accomplishments separated from disputed claims and later legend?
- Human Art, Regalia, Legacy, capability, and limitation established?
- Opponent contrast and unanswered danger visible?
- Introduction dramatized rather than delivered as a biography dump?

## Foreshadowing, Twist, and Antagonist
- Seed category, first interpretation, reinforcement, reveal, payoff, and consequence recorded where required?
- Major twist passes all fair-play and consequence gates?
- Antagonist has an understandable motive and specific threatened value?
- Antagonistic pressure changes a choice, relationship, resource, safety, public belief, or Crown knowledge?

## Prose
- Close third-person limited is maintained by default?
- Character voice is distinct and conversation feels human?
- Emotion is embodied through perception, action, dialogue, and choice?
- Repeated AI-pattern constructions and over-explanation are absent?

## National Collapse
- Consequences visible and varied?
- Civilians/refugees treated as people rather than abstract stakes?
- Losing champion responds emotionally where appropriate?

## Endgame
- New Crown lore preserves earlier clues?
- Crown War grows from established relationships?
- Shift from national survival to resistance is morally and causally earned?

If any answer exposes a contradiction, fix the plan before prose production continues.
