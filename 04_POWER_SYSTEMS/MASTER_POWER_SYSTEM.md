# WORLD CROWN — Master Power System

## Core Principle
Every champion is their own power system. There are no universal RPG classes and no requirement that fighters use the same supernatural energy. A champion's combat identity must emerge from who they were, how they fought, what they achieved, how history remembers them, and what their Crown Title means.

# 1. Human Art
Everything the champion genuinely knew or developed in life: swordsmanship, boxing, wrestling, archery, spear fighting, martial arts, horsemanship, marksmanship, battlefield tactics, footwork, timing, survival, leadership, weapon mastery, and similar real competencies.

Human Art must remain relevant even in late fights. Supernatural abilities extend skill rather than replace it.

# 2. Crown Body - Host-Synced State
The World Crown does not resurrect or reconstruct the champion's true body. For a deceased or historical champion, Crown Body is the living host's temporarily synchronized combat state: the Crown enhances the host while the champion's memory-pattern, trained instincts, and identity become accessible through Host Sync. Living champions may enter in a self-hosted state.

Specialties may include strength, speed, durability, stamina, reactions, precision, senses, and recovery, but the host's physical history and limits remain relevant. Host and champion do not become one unquestioned person. Incomplete alignment can cause latency, pain, memory intrusion, control conflict, or desync. The host's body is not a neutral container, and the sync must be earned through compatibility, negotiation, or shared conviction.

# 3. Regalia
A champion's supernatural combat equipment, derived from documented weapons, strongly associated equipment, fighting tradition, or symbolic objects central to legend. If an exact historical weapon is unknown, the story must not falsely present an invented weapon as authenticated fact.

Every important Regalia should have a distinct property tied to identity: storing momentum, altering range, changing form under conditions, redirecting force, strengthening while defending, interacting with terrain, and similar rules.

## True Regalia
Optional, not universal. Must be foreshadowed and tied to character development or Crown Resonance.

# 4. Legacy Trait
A passive supernatural characteristic reflecting enduring reputation: survival, dueling perception, battlefield awareness, endurance, tracking, leadership, adaptation, psychological pressure, etc.

# 5. Signature Arts
Named techniques created from Human Art + Crown Body + Regalia + Legacy. A fully developed champion usually has around 3–7. They may be attacks, counters, movement, defense, traps, stance changes, terrain methods, or tactical sequences. No unrelated spectacle attacks.

# 6. Crown Title
A unique epithet condensing what history, culture, and legend most strongly associate with the champion. Avoid repetitive generic titles unless genuinely justified.

# 7. Legend Authority
The highest rule generated by the Crown Title. It is not simply a finishing move; it changes combat logic in a way that reflects the legend.

Every Authority must define:
- Power
- Condition
- Limitation
- Counterplay

No Authority may simply mean “this champion wins.”

# 8. Crown Resonance
Growth is alignment among the host's true self, the champion's historical life, public legend, Crown Title, and present conviction. For self-hosted living champions, the host/champion distinction is internalized rather than represented by two separate people.

- Low Resonance — mainly Human Art + Crown Body.
- Awakened Resonance — Regalia, Legacy, and Signature Arts become more complete.
- High Resonance — Legend Authority is reliable.
- Perfect Resonance — the champion reconciles true self and inherited legend.

# 9. Legend Distortion
Public memory can exaggerate, distort, glorify, villainize, or mythologize the real person. The Crown can manifest powers based partly on that later memory. This is a character conflict, not permission to ignore historical research.

# 10. Crown Revelation
A rare climax state when Human Art, Crown Title, Legacy, and present conviction align. It can manifest as True Regalia, battlefield change, perfected technique, altered body state, temporary conceptual rule, or irreversible sacrifice. It must be earned and foreshadowed.

# Balancing Rules
- Fame does not equal power.
- Era does not determine victory.
- Matchups matter.
- Every power needs counterplay.
- Experience persists through knowledge and new applications of established abilities.
- History should foreshadow power direction.
- Avoid generic energy escalation.

# Standard Power Profile
Every champion file must define Host Sync or self-hosted status, host identity and agency, compatibility, sync conflict/cost, Human Art, and Crown Body specialty in addition to the power layers below.
Every champion file must define Human Art, Crown Body specialty, Regalia/property, Legacy Trait, 3–7 Signature Arts in full canon, Crown Title, Legend Authority, condition, limitation, counterplay, Crown Resonance conflict, optional True Regalia/Revelation, and primary weakness.
